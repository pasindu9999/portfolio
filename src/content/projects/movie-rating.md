The final year research project behind my BSc thesis: an AI-driven system that
classifies movie content against age-appropriateness guidelines, so that rating
decisions can be supported by evidence from the footage itself rather than by
manual review alone.

## Approach

Built a deep-learning model using **YOLOv8** for human action recognition in
video clips, detecting the specific categories that drive an age rating.

I personally developed the **substance-abuse detection sub-module**.

## Context

This work formed the basis of my final year thesis, *"Automated Movie Content
Rating System"*, submitted toward a BSc. (Hons) in Information Technology at the
University of Moratuwa.
