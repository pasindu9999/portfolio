A web application built to streamline the employee appraisal process and reduce
the manual workload it put on HR.

## What it does

The system specialises in two linked pieces of the appraisal cycle:

- **Employee rating** — capturing and structuring performance assessment
- **Performance-based salary increments** — deriving increment decisions from
  those ratings rather than handling them as a separate manual exercise

## Context

Second year research project at the University of Moratuwa, built on Angular,
ASP.NET and MS SQL Server.
